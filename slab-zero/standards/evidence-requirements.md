# Evidence Requirements

Every promotion candidate must bind: claim_id, exact claim, repository identity, commit SHA, evidence digest, test result, evidence locator, verification identity, policy version, and provenance proof.

Alpha verification must additionally record authority, exact authoritative scope, record/certificate identifier, retrieval time, and any uncovered scope. Official evidence paths should be followed when accessible. Anything outside the authority's stated scope requires independent system verification.

Images and UI captures are evidence artifacts when they are hashed, preserved, described, and bound to the exact build/commit they represent. Descriptions never replace the original artifact.
